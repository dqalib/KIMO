# Research summary (Sept 2026)

## Market
| Product | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kumon (centre) | Varies by centre + registration fee | Paper or Kumon Connect tablet+stylus; 2 visits/week + daily homework |
| Komodo | ~£9.99/mo | Closest rival: Kumon-style little-and-often, UK, ages 5–11, maths + English |
| DoodleMaths/English | ~£7.99/mo | Gamified, adaptive |
| Maths-Whizz | ~£20/mo | Best parent reporting |
| IXL | ~£79/yr per subject | Huge bank, US feel |
| Times Tables Rock Stars | ~£14 / 3 yrs | Times tables only |

**Positioning:** app + in-person (club/tutor) blend. Target ~£8–12/mo app-only; higher for in-person tier.

## Kumon Connect
Tablet + stylus (iPad 10th gen, Samsung S6 Lite). Worksheets auto-delivered in order; instructor marks remotely and adjusts study plan; parents see results.

## Statutory hooks
- Year 1: Phonics Screening Check (June, statutory)
- Year 2: KS1 tests optional since 2023/24
- Year 4: Multiplication Tables Check (June, statutory)

## ICO Children's Code — key standards
Best interests of the child; DPIA; age-appropriate application; transparency; no detrimental data use; high-privacy defaults; data minimisation; limited data sharing; geolocation off; parental controls transparency; profiling off by default; no nudge techniques; online tools for exercising rights.

## Stack costs
Supabase Free: 50k MAU, 500 MB DB, pauses after 1 week inactive. Pro: $25/mo, 8 GB DB, no pausing.
